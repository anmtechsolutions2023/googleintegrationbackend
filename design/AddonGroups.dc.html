Add-on Groups

A group is a question you ask about a dish. The add-ons under it are the answers.

Name Code Min Max Options Active
Extra Toppings TOPPINGS 0 3 4 Yes
Choose your bread BREAD 1 1 3 Yes
Spice level SPICE 1 1 3 Yes
Min 0 = optional
“Extra Toppings” can be skipped entirely.
Min 1 = the customer must pick
Bread and spice level are required before the dish can be ordered.
Min above Max is refused
“Pick at least 3, at most 1” can never be satisfied — you get a clear message, not a failed order later.

No onions Extra Toppings ₹ 0.00 Veg Yes
Why an add-on carries its own Food Type

A veg pizza with a chicken topping is not a veg order. The dietary badge on the add-on is the only thing that can say so — the dish’s own Veg mark would keep claiming otherwise. “No onions” at ₹0 is a legitimate option: it costs nothing but the kitchen still has to be told.
