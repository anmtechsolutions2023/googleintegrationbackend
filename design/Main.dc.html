Six new screens, two extended

Everything below is reachable in the app today. The six marked NEW are new sidebar entries; Menu Master and Online Orders gained fields on screens you already use.

Meat Types
/frontdesk/meat-types

Chicken, Mutton, Fish… Separate from Veg/Non-Veg, not a replacement: a dish is Non-Veg and Chicken. A diner avoiding pork filters on this, and “Non-Veg” alone cannot answer them.

Menu Tags
/frontdesk/menu-tags

One list, three kinds — Category, Beverage, Cuisine. A tag picker only ever offers its own kind, so the beverage list never shows cuisines.

Add-on Groups → Add-ons
two screens

A group is a question (“Extra toppings?”) with a min/max; add-ons are its answers. Not the same as a Variant — a variant replaces the price, an add-on adds to it.

Category Hours
/frontdesk/category-schedules

When a section is on the menu — “Breakfast disappears at 11”. A category with no hours is always available, so nothing you have today changes until you set one.

Rejection Reasons
/frontdesk/rejection-reasons

Why an order was refused, from a list instead of free text — so “why did we reject 40 orders last week?” has an answer.

Sub-categories
Master Data → Categories

Categories can now sit under a parent — Mains → Curries. Exactly two levels; the form refuses a third, because portals reject deeper menus.

Two screens you already use gained fields

Menu Master — meat type, add-on groups, tags, serves, portion size, prep time, nutrition, and a GST goods/service flag. Online Orders — a prep-time promise on accept, and the customer’s cooking instructions now reaching the kitchen ticket.
