The distinction that matters most on this form

A Variant replaces the price — Half is ₹180 instead of ₹320. An Add-on adds to it — Extra Chicken is ₹80 on top. Portals check add-on quantities against the group’s min/max; a variant has no such pair, which is why modelling add-ons as variants is where this integration usually goes wrong.
